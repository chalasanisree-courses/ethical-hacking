# Syllabus

## Course description

Students will scan, test, hack, and secure systems; implement perimeter defenses; and scan and attack virtual networks. Coverage includes intrusion detection, social engineering, footprinting, DDoS attacks, buffer overflows, SQL injection, privilege escalation, trojans, backdoors, and wireless hacking, with emphasis on legal and ethical guidelines. The course prepares students for the **Certified Ethical Hacker (C\|EH)** exam.

## Course objectives

1.  Explore ethical hacking fundamentals
2.  Explore cryptographic concepts
3.  Investigate reconnaissance and information gathering
4.  Explore scanning and enumeration
5.  Explore network hacking via sniffers and evasion
6.  Investigate system attacks
7.  Explore low-tech hacking techniques
8.  Investigate web-based hacking
9.  Explore wireless network hacking
10. Investigate trojans and related attacks
11. Perform penetration testing

## Academic integrity & ethics

The skills in this course are powerful and, misused, illegal. You will practice them **only** in the provided lab environment or against systems you have **explicit written authorization** to test. Any attack against systems, networks, or accounts without authorization is a violation of course policy and of state and federal law, and may result in course failure and possible referral. When in doubt, ask before you act.
